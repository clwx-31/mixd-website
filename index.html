What we do

Collaboration, without the wait

Big brand collabs normally take a year and a legal team. We licence smaller, well-loved labels and put the drop out in six weeks.

🤝

We licence the brand

We sign short-term licensing deals with heritage snack, drink and record labels — the ones people already feel nostalgic about.

✏️

We design the mix

Our in-house team blends the partner's identity with the MIXD silhouette. Every drop is one design, released in one run.

📦

We drop it and stop

250 pieces, then the design is retired for good. Scarcity is the product as much as the cotton is.

How it works

From handshake to hoodie in six weeks

1

Partner

We approach a brand with a loyal following and agree a short licence.

2

Design

One design per partner. Approved by both sides in two rounds.

3

Produce

250 units printed by our certified supplier on organic cotton blanks.

4

Drop

Announced 48 hours ahead. Sold online only. Never restocked.

Why limited runs

Making less, on purpose

The clothing industry's biggest problem is unsold stock. By producing a fixed 250 units and never restocking, MIXD sells through almost everything it makes — which is better for our margin and better for the landfill.

Organic & recycled only

Every blank is GOTS-certified organic cotton or recycled poly-cotton.

Printed to order region

Produced in the region it ships to, cutting freight emissions per unit.

Under 4% unsold

Leftover pieces are donated rather than destroyed or discounted.

Don't miss the next drop

Drop 15 is announced 48 hours before it goes live. Subscribers get first access.
