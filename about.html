About MIXD

Designed for the unexpected

The best collaborations feel surprising, inevitable and impossible to repeat.

Our story

It started with a joke

In 2024 our founders printed a single hoodie mixing a local root beer label with a skate brand, purely as a birthday gift. It was photographed, posted, and within a week eleven strangers had asked where to buy one.

There was nowhere to buy one. That gap became the business. MIXD now licences well-loved but under-used brands — snack labels, record shops, transit authorities, sunscreen companies — and turns each one into a single, limited apparel drop.

We are deliberately small. One design per partner, 250 pieces per run, and then it is gone. That constraint is what keeps the brand desirable and keeps our unsold stock close to zero.

Mission statement

What we do today

“To make collaboration culture affordable and sustainable by turning well-loved brands into limited-run, responsibly made apparel that our customers are proud to wear and never see everywhere.”

Vision statement

Where we are going

“To become the collaboration partner of choice for independent brands worldwide — proving that a fashion label can grow without producing a single garment it cannot sell.”

Our values

What we will not compromise on

🎯

Scarcity, not hype

We limit runs because it reduces waste — the hype is a side effect, not the goal.

🌱

Materials first

Certified organic or recycled fabric on every product, with no exceptions.

⚖️

Fair to partners

Every licensing partner receives a royalty on each unit, not a flat fee.

💬

Honest pricing

We publish what each garment costs us to make and what we keep.
