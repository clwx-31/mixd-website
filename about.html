Our story

Four friends. One shared idea.

MIXD was founded in Waukesha, Wisconsin, in 2026 by Jack, Massimo, Dillon and Ben. As young adults looking for a way to build something of their own, they started with a shared interest in clothing, styling and thrifting.

They saw that people often had to shop across many places to create the look they wanted. MIXD brings versatile pieces together in one simple online experience, making it easier to mix brands and build a personal wardrobe.

The goal is straightforward: offer quality clothing, listen closely to customers and keep improving the choices available to them.

Proposed nonprofit partner

Hope Center helps people in Waukesha County meet basic needs including food, shelter and clothing. That makes it a strong local fit for a company built around clothing and customer community.
