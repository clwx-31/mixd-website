FAQ

Asked often

When is the next drop?

Drops land roughly every six weeks. The email drop list always gets the date first.

Will a sold-out piece come back?

No. Every collaboration is produced once and never reprinted. The core range is always available.

How long does shipping take?

Three to five working days in Canada and the US, seven to ten in Europe. Pre-orders ship on the stated date.

Can I return something?

Yes — unworn items within 30 days, tags attached. Return postage is on us if the fit is wrong.

How do I pitch my brand?

Email partners@mixd.example with what your brand is and why the mix would work.

Do you sell wholesale?

Only to a small number of stockists. Send your shop details and we will get back to you.
