FAQ

Asked often

What does MIXD sell?

Hoodies, shirts, pants, shorts, underwear, socks, beanies, crewnecks and tank tops.

What makes MIXD different?

Our versatile pieces are designed to mix across brands, giving customers more control over their clothing choices.

How does the six-month warranty work?

Qualifying material or construction problems are covered for six months under the final warranty terms. Contact us with the order and product details.

Where is MIXD based?

MIXD was founded in 2026 in Waukesha, Wisconsin, and is designed as an online-first company.

Which nonprofit collaborates with MIXD?

MIXD collaborates with Goodwill Greater Milwaukee & Chicago through clothing drives and responsible donation awareness. Visit the Goodwill Waukesha website.

Does customer feedback matter?

Yes. Feedback about fit, material, comfort and selection directly guides future product improvements.
