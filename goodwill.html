Why this partnership matters

Clothing can do more.

MIXD is built around quality clothing, personal expression and thoughtful choices. Goodwill turns donated goods and store purchases into support for employment training and job placement programs.

That creates a natural connection. Instead of treating unwanted clothing as waste, our collaboration encourages customers to keep useful pieces in circulation and create value for the wider community.

Local

Goodwill operates a store and donation center at 1400 Nike Drive in Waukesha.

Relevant

Reuse and responsible donation directly connect to MIXD’s clothing business.

Actionable

Customers can participate through clear, convenient clothing-donation opportunities.

How we collaborate

Simple actions. Measurable impact.

Our partnership focuses on practical programs that customers can understand and join.

01

Community clothing drives

MIXD organizes collection events for clean, wearable clothing and directs donors to Goodwill’s local donation center.

02

Donation awareness

Our website and social channels explain how responsible donation extends product life and supports Goodwill’s mission.

03

Impact tracking

Each campaign records participation and donated-item totals so the team can evaluate and improve future drives.

Shared values

A collaboration that fits MIXD.

Goodwill gives our three core values a visible purpose beyond the product.

Quality

Durable clothing stays useful longer. We encourage customers to care for garments and donate wearable pieces when their needs change.

Collaboration

MIXD, Goodwill and our customers each play a role in keeping clothing in use and strengthening local opportunity.

Customer focus

We give customers a convenient, positive next step for clothing they no longer wear instead of sending it to waste.

How customers participate

Wear it. Share it. Keep it moving.

The idea is intentionally simple: choose clothing carefully, use it well, then help it reach another person when it no longer belongs in your wardrobe.

1. Choose well

Build a wardrobe around versatile MIXD pieces that work across different looks and brands.

2. Wear longer

Use the six-month warranty when needed and care for each garment to extend its useful life.

3. Donate responsibly

Bring clean, wearable clothing to a MIXD drive or directly to the Goodwill Waukesha donation center.
